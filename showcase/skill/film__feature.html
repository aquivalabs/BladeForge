see it

fires when

Use when someone asks for a presentation film, video briefing or animated walkthrough of one specific feature or change, built from its spec, plan, code or ticket, to show a team, stakeholders or a client why it exists, how it works and what is still open, and made in Claude Motion. Do NOT use for a promo or marketing video, a generic animation or a skill's showcase hover film (plain Claude Motion), for a slide deck (the Slides type), for a clickable code-architecture diagram (diagram:diagram), or for a how-to tutorial.

best for

Authors who need stakeholders to understand why a feature exists, see one real request go through it, and know what is proven and what is still open — without a promo or a slide deck.
