cerberus:leak-check v2.1.5

Agent judgment pass that reviews a skill/reference/script/eval change — and, before a push, the outgoing commits' messages, authors, committers and branch name — for anything pointing to a real work codebase (secrets, real people/emails/paths, employer or client brand, real namespace/object names, or the aggregate "one company's product" flavor) and rewrites it to a neutral fictional demo.

fires when

Use before committing OR PUSHING a NEW or EDITED skill, its references, its bundled scripts, or its eval fixtures to this PUBLIC marketplace — review the change, and before a push also the outgoing commits themselves (messages, author, committer, branch name), for anything that points to a real work codebase (real class/object/namespace/org/ticket names, secrets, real people/emails/paths, an employer or client brand, or the aggregate "this is one specific company's product" flavor) and rewrite it to a neutral fictional demo before it ships. Also runs when the CERBERUS hook flags a skill/script/eval edit.

best for

Any contributor to this public skills marketplace, right before committing or pushing a new or edited skill, reference, or eval fixture — also triggered automatically by the cerberus PostToolUse hook on skill edits, and asked for by the cerberus push guard when it blocks a push.
